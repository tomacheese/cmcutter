## Compile JLSE

Uses [JoinLogoScpTrialSet](https://github.com/tobitti0/JoinLogoScpTrialSetLinux), a port of join_logo_scp that runs on Linux.
Docker is recommended, but this setup runs it directly without virtualization.

For hardware encoding (the `h264_v4l2m2m` encoder used in `getJLSECommand`), see:

- https://www.thun-techblog.com/index.php/blog/join-logo-scp-on-armlinux/
